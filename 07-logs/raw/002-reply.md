**Positions the mentor took (full reasoning in `00-context/mentor-charter.md`):**

1. Twelve weeks builds a foundation, not mastery of five styles. Real money goes only into cash-equity swing trades; futures, options and order flow are learned deeply and practised on paper.
2. The worst loss (a leveraged short locked in circuit, 30% of capital in one trade) is the central lesson. The rules are built to make that impossible: size from the stop, no leverage, no shorting in cash, liquid stocks only.
3. Real trading stops at a 10% drawdown, not 20%. At 1% risk that is ten straight full losses, which means something is broken.
4. No real-money F&O during the course. At Nifty lot size 65, neither a futures lot nor a typical spread fits a 1% risk rule on ₹1 lakh.
5. No new real trades in weeks 1–2. Real trades start after Gate 1 at the end of week 3, at 0.5% risk.
6. The general loss statistic will not be repeated. Specific risks will still be flagged whenever a trade, a size or a rule calls for it.
7. Short straddles and strangles are not "low risk": high win rate and small maximum loss are different things. Week 8 tests this with numbers.
