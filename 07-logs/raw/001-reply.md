Three things I'd flag now:

- **Leave:** don't take earned leave for this yet. In the first three months the bottleneck is screen time and repetition over many weeks, not hours per day. A daily 2–3 hours is enough, and the leave is worth more later for a focused block of live trading once you have a tested setup.
- **Order flow:** I'd put it in month three, not month one. It only makes sense once you read market structure and auction logic well; before that it's just colourful noise on a chart.
- **Your edge:** as far as I know, SEBI's own studies show roughly nine in ten individual F&O traders lose money. Faster indicators won't beat that. What your CS background gives you is the ability to test every idea on data before risking money, and to journal and measure yourself honestly, so I intend to build the roadmap around that.
