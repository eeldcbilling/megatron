# Risk rules

Proposed by the mentor on 13 Nov 2026. **Status: awaiting the learner's written acceptance** (see [`open-questions.md`](open-questions.md)). Once accepted, they change only by a dated entry in [`decisions.md`](decisions.md), written outside market hours and never during an open trade.

Trading capital for the course: **₹1,00,000** in one broker account used for nothing else. The second lakh stays out until the week-12 review.

## Position rules

| # | Rule |
|---|---|
| R1 | Risk per trade is at most **0.5% of capital (₹500) in weeks 3–4** and **1% (₹1,000) from week 5**, if journal compliance is 90% or better. |
| R2 | Quantity = risk amount ÷ (entry price − stop price). Size comes from the stop, never from available funds. |
| R3 | The stop-loss is decided before entry and placed as an order (GTT) right after the buy fills. It is never widened. It may be tightened. |
| R4 | At most 2 open positions in month 1, at most 3 after that. |
| R5 | No leverage of any kind: no MIS, no MTF, no short-selling in cash, no real-money futures or options during these 12 weeks. |
| R6 | Only liquid stocks from the Nifty 200. Nothing that has hit a circuit limit in the last month. |
| R7 | No trade from a tip, a channel, a friend or a news headline. Every trade comes from the written system. |
| R8 | Every trade is written in the journal **before** entry: setup, entry, stop, target, quantity, reason. No entry in the journal, no trade. |

## Circuit breakers

| Trigger | Action |
|---|---|
| 2 losing trades in one week | No new trades until the weekend review |
| Capital down 6% in a calendar month | Paper only for the rest of that month |
| Capital down 10% from the start (₹90,000) | Real trading stops. Full review with the mentor before anything resumes |
| A rule is broken, even on a winning trade | Logged as a violation. Three violations in a month: paper only for two weeks |

## Pre-trade checklist

Answer in the journal, in writing:

1. Which setup from my system is this?
2. Where exactly am I wrong, and is the stop order ready?
3. What is the quantity by R2?
4. Is the reward at least twice the risk?
5. Am I rested and calm, or am I trying to recover something?

A "no" or a blank on any line means no trade.

## Worked example of R2

Capital ₹1,00,000, risk 1% = ₹1,000. Stock at ₹500, stop at ₹475, so risk per share is ₹25.
Quantity = 1,000 ÷ 25 = **40 shares**, a position of ₹20,000. If the stop is hit the loss is about ₹1,000 plus costs.

## Paper trading

Futures and options are paper-traded (Sensibull virtual trading or an equivalent) with the same journal discipline. A paper trade without a journal entry does not count toward any gate.
