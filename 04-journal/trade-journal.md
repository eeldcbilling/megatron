# Trade journal

One block per trade, newest at the bottom. The "before" part is written before the order is placed (rule R8).

## Template

```
### T-000 | SYMBOL | real or paper | system setup name

BEFORE (date, time)
- Trend (weekly / daily):
- Setup and trigger:
- Entry:            Stop:            Target:
- Risk per share:   Risk amount:     Quantity (R2):
- Reward-to-risk:
- Why now, in one sentence:
- What would prove me wrong before the stop:
- Checklist passed (5 of 5)?

AFTER (date)
- Exit price and reason:
- Result in R:
- Did I follow every rule? If not, which one:
- What the market did that I did not expect:
- Screenshot or chart note:
```
