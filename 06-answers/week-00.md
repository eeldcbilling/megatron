What is missing: the question asked for a rule, and this is a description of a feeling. "Do not be stupid or overconfident" cannot be followed, because nobody feels stupid at the moment of entry. 1. **Size.** The position was three times the capital. With size set from a stop at 1% risk, the same bad idea costs 1%.
2. **Instrument.** A short in the cash market, in a stock that can lock in upper circuit, cannot be exited once it locks. No stop-loss works there.

A well-reasoned short in the same stock at the same size would have lost exactly the same amount. That is why the rules do not try to make you smarter; they make being wrong cheap.
